# Attribution and source restoration

This package reuses the public plby/lean-proofs snapshot 8822f7ddef30fadbd92e1c6ab4ed897af356af5e. Mathematical authors are listed separately in each problem document. Upstream formal-author headers variously identify Codex, GPT-5.6 Sol, Aristotle Contributors, Boris Alexeev and Formal Conjectures Authors. The Bernoulli interface used by Erdos920 comes from a source crediting Claude and Pawan Sasanka Ammanamanchi. Reconstruction preserves every original notice.

CHENLexiao8848 directed this AI-assisted project and contributes local interfaces, semantic equivalences, Lean4.34 compatibility repairs, a Chebyshev-only prime-block estimate for Erdos808, and reproducibility/verification materials. The submitter is not automatically the original solver or author of reused proofs. This is not independent human review or an award announcement.

Third-party proof bodies are not redistributed in this package. sources.json pins public source URLs, a full commit, upstream hashes, exact line edits and final hashes. restore_sources.py downloads and restores the tested closure. Some files have explicit Apache2 notices and others have no individual license header; no blanket relicensing or redistribution permission is asserted. The repository root license does not assert ownership of downloaded third-party material.

Erdos808 retains the complete disproof of the strong conjecture, but omits the separate ancillary incidence lower bound. Its PNT-dependent coarse prime bound is replaced with a proved local Chebyshev bound. Erdos920 retains its complete geometric construction and uses only the proved finite Bernoulli interface from the unrelated large Erdos202 development. Extraction rules are explicit and exact output hashes are checked.

JSP-000688 is excluded while the workbook's fixed-three-color wording and the linked large-k problem remain unresolved.
