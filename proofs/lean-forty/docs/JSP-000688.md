# JSP-000688 / Erdos832

[Original question](https://www.erdosproblems.com/832).

## Complete scope

The original eventual binomial lower-bound conjecture is false. For every chromatic threshold K there are k>=K and a finite simple 4-uniform hypergraph H with chromatic number exactly k and fewer than binomial(3(k-1)+1,4) edges. Thus the lower bound itself fails, not merely its equality characterization. The proof preserves the original quantifier order and is not the fixed r=k=3 Fano-plane example.

The positive assertion quantified over all ranks r>=3 and all sufficiently large chromatic numbers for each rank. A fixed rank four with cofinally large exact chromatic numbers disproves it completely. The rank-three asymptotic branch, the fixed-three-color minimum-edge problem and the separate later quantitative bounds are not claimed.

The upstream finite hypergraph has a finite set of finite vertex subsets as its edge family, so repeated edges are excluded. Uniformity requires exactly four vertices in each edge. Exact chromatic number includes k-colorability and failure of every smaller palette. A binary bilinear construction and an edge-minimal subhypergraph reduction prove strict counterexamples of arbitrarily large exact chromatic number; this is not just a small witness or conditional implication.
