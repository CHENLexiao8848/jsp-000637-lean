# JSP-000640 / Erdos 780

[Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/780).

## Complete scope

For all n,k and positive r,t, n >= kr+(t-1)(k-1) forces k distinct, pairwise disjoint edges of one color in every t-coloring of all r-subsets. Composite k and threshold equality are included.
