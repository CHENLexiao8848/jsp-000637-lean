# JSP-000665 / Erdos 808

[Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/808).

## Complete scope

The strong graph-restricted sum-product conjecture is false. For c=29/34 and epsilon=1/68 there are arbitrarily large finite sets of distinct positive natural labels with at least n^(63/34) edges but restricted sum/product cardinalities below n^(125/68). A Chebyshev prime-block lemma replaces the external PNT dependency. The separate background incidence lower bound is not claimed.
