# JSP-000689 / Erdos 833

[Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/833).

## Complete scope

For every r>=2, every finite r-uniform hypergraph of chromatic number exactly three has a vertex incident to at least (10/9)^r distinct edges. The Erdos-Lovasz bound 2^(r-1)/(4r) is also proved for all non-two-colorable such hypergraphs, including small ranks.
