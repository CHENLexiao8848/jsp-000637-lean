# JSP-000655 / Erdos 797

[Original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/797).

## Complete scope

Full AMR upper and lower bounds for maximal acyclic chromatic number f(d), in exact cube form, and f(d)=o(d^2). Every sufficiently large d is covered. The coloring predicate is equivalent to proper coloring with every pair of color classes inducing a forest.
