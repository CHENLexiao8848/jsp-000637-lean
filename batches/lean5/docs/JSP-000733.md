# JSP-000733 / Erdős 882

This is a complete implementation of the leading asymptotic in the [original problem](https://www.erdosproblems.com/882). It completes the selected ToshiDad lower-bound core, while the same full conclusion was already publicly formalized in [PR #31](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/pull/31). ## Statement correspondence

Entry: [LeanTwenty/JSP000733.lean](../LeanTwenty/JSP000733.lean), theorem `JSP000733.solution`.

- `PrimitiveSums A` says that divisibility between two nonempty subset-sum values forces equality of those values. It does not assume injectivity of the subset-sum map.
- `Admissible n A` also requires `A ⊆ Finset.Icc 1 n`. `maximumSize n` is the actual maximum cardinality over every such admissible set; `maximumSize_attained` proves attainment. The empty set ensures the defining family is nonempty, including at `n = 0`.
- `subset_sums_injective` derives injectivity for all subsets, including the empty subset, from admissibility. `maximum_counting_bound` gives `2 ^ maximumSize n ≤ maximumSize n * n + 1`.
- `lower_bound` gives `Nat.log 2 n ≤ maximumSize n` for `0 < n`. Together with the counting bound, `solution` proves that `maximumSize(n) / log₂(n)` tends to `1` as `n` tends to infinity. No positive-`n` hypothesis is added to the final limit theorem.

The result is the complete leading asymptotic, not an exact extremal formula or a half-log-log or bounded-additive-error refinement. Its hypotheses do not assume the desired distinctness or introduce an unproved mathematical statement.
