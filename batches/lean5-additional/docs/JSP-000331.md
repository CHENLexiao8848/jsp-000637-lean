# JSP-000331 / Erdős 402: selected eventual statement

The [official JSP-000331 entry](https://github.com/TheJustinSunPrize/awards/blob/82be4c4913b8fe394d68d1391f4c221fde947211/problems/catalog-0301-0400.md#JSP-000331) explicitly asks about every sufficiently large finite integer set. [Erdős 402](https://www.erdosproblems.com/402) also describes the stronger all-cardinality Graham problem; that extension is not asserted here.

JSP000331.solution states: there exists a natural N0 >= 2 such that every finite set A of natural numbers, with |A| >= N0 and 0 not in A, has distinct a,b in A with gcd(a,b) <= a/|A| over the rationals. Every set above the single uniform threshold is covered, with no normalization, squarefree, prime-cardinality or analytic hypothesis on A. The threshold is non-effective.

The [wrapper](../LeanTwenty/JSP000331.lean) applies the proved eventual theorem Erdos402.erdos_402 and derives distinctness by raising N0 to max(2,N). The 138-declaration extraction preserves the retained mathematical statements. Finite native_decide certificates and the unresolved finite bridge belong to the stronger all-cardinality development and lie outside this theorem's dependency closure.
